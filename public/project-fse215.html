E4 Förbifart FSE 215

Years 2019–2023

The team’s experience also includes earlier roles with other employers.

Client Need

Trafikverket is building a comprehensive infrastructure project along the E4 to support development in a rapidly growing Stockholm region.

Project Overview

Infinity Mätkonsult participated in building concrete tunnels, road widenings, ponds, walls and asphalt works, enabling around 100,000 vehicles per day to pass in the future.

Surveying the construction works

Surveying supports construction from excavation through tunnel works. Precision instruments help teams check positions and levels on site.

Excavation and retaining structures

Road widening and excavation works require careful coordination around the existing road and temporary retaining structures.

Tunnel construction

The project included concrete tunnel works at different stages, from structural reinforcement to the construction of the tunnel lining.

Surveying around active infrastructure

Work around tunnel approaches and existing traffic calls for accurate measurements coordinated with ongoing construction activity.

Excavation overview

The aerial view shows the scale of the excavation and the retaining structures used at the site.

Challenges

Widening of the existing roads, two concrete tunnels (one on each side of the existing E20 road at the Förbifart connection), emergency exits, rock tunnels, rock wire cutting, comprehensive ground reinforcement work such as sheet piles, curtain grouting, rock blasting and rock bolting, excavation of polluted soil, sewage and water/wastewater (VA) systems, and cycle paths.

Project FSE 215 Executed Quantities

Rock Excavation: 70,000 m³
Soil Excavation: 30,000 m³
Concrete: 20,000 m³
