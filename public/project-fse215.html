E4 Förbifart FSE 215

Year 2019-2023

Client Need

Trafikverket is building a comprehensive infrastructure project in E4 to support the development in a rapidly growing Stockholm region.

Project Overview

Infinity Mätkonsult participated in the building of concrete tunnels, widenings, ponds, walls, asphalt works, to enable in the future the circulation of around 100,000 vehicles per day.

Challenges

Widening of the existing roads, two concrete tunnels (one on each side of E20), Emergency exits, rock tunnels, rock wire cutting, comprehensive ground reinforcement work as sheet piles, curtain grouting, rock blasting, rock bolting, polluted soil excavation, sewage system and VA, bike trails.

Project FSE 215 Executed Quantities

Rock Excavation: 70,000 m³
Soil Excavation: 30,000 m³
Concrete: 20,000 m³
