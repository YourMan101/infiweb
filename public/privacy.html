Privacy notice

This notice explains how Infinity Mätkonsult AB handles personal information submitted through the contact form.

Who is responsible?

Infinity Mätkonsult AB (organisation number 559397-3190) is responsible for the personal information. You can contact us at info@infinitymk.se or Månskärsvägen 10B, 141 75 Kungens Kurva, Sweden.

What information do we collect and why?

The form may collect your name, email address, phone number, company, preferred working arrangement, service of interest and project description. We use this information to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, discuss or prepare a potential assignment. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering a contract, or our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Who processes the information?

Form submissions are sent to our email inbox. Vercel provides website hosting; Formspree receives the form and forwards it by email; Microsoft provides the Outlook mailbox where we receive it. These providers process information as part of their services. Please review their privacy information: Vercel, Formspree and Microsoft.

Depending on provider configuration, information may be processed outside the European Economic Area. Please refer to the providers’ privacy information and applicable service terms for details about international processing.

How long do we keep it?

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond and follow up. If it becomes part of a business relationship, we may keep relevant records for as long as necessary to manage that relationship or meet legal obligations.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or erase your personal information, or ask us to restrict or stop certain processing. Contact us at info@infinitymk.se. You can also lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
