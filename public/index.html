Our Services

Data Extract from Design and Models

Extracting precise data from design specifications and 3D models for accurate project implementation.

Setting Out Data

Setting out data from design specifications for precise construction layout and positioning.

As-Built Measurements

Data measurements for as-built drawings to ensure accuracy and compliance with design specifications.

Network Measurements

Network measurements and maintenance for reliable project foundations and ongoing monitoring.

Control Measurements

Control measurements for settlement monitoring to track structural integrity and ground movement.

3D Modeling for Machine Control

Advanced 3D modeling for machine control operations ensuring precision in automated construction processes.

Quantities Calculation

Drone Technology

Orthophoto maps and photos using drone technology for comprehensive aerial surveying and documentation.

Stock Pile Volumes

Quantities estimation based on drone flights for accurate stock pile volume calculations and monitoring.

Documentation & Payment Certificates

Comprehensive documentation and monthly payment certificates works for project management and financial tracking.
