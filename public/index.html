About Us

Founded in 2022, Infinity Mätkonsult AB is a consultancy firm specializing in advanced surveying solutions for infrastructure projects.

Our team consists of experts with over a decade of experience in various fields, providing a wealth of knowledge, values, and technical proficiency to every project.

We uphold the highest standards of accuracy and efficiency in the construction and development of infrastructure.
