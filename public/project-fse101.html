E4 Förbifart FSE 101

Project · 2023–2026

Client Needs

Trafikverket through our direct client is building a comprehensive infrastructure project in Förbifart Stockholm to support the development in a rapidly growing Stockholm region.

Infinity Mätkonsult AB continues to support the client for building the E4 infrastructure project to enable the circulation of around 100,000 vehicles per day.

Surveying and setting out

Surveying supports accurate positioning and level checks throughout road and bridge construction, helping the site teams build to the design.

Excavation and retaining works

Road widening and excavation works take place alongside existing infrastructure. Surveying helps coordinate levels and positions as the works progress.

Bridge construction

Bridge works progress through several stages, from formwork and casting to the completed structure. Measurements help maintain the required geometry throughout construction.

Completed bridges and road connections

The project includes new bridge connections and a roundabout designed to link the road network and keep traffic moving through the area.

Works alongside live traffic

Construction and surveying are coordinated with traffic using the existing roads. Some activities are carried out at night or in winter conditions.

Challenges

Building of roads and bridge in Vårby, widening of the existing roads, build of roundabout bridge for traffic and pedestrians, built retaining walls, soil excavation, rock excavation and blasting, building of a new pedestrian and bike road, and many traffic diversions in order to keep the current traffic ongoing.

Project FSE 101 Executed Quantities

Rock Excavation: 40,000 m³
Soil Excavation: 130,000 m³
Concrete: 20,000 m³

Services Provided by Infinity Mätkonsult AB

  • Data extract from design and models
  • Setting out data from design specifications
  • Measurements for as-built drawings
  • Network measurements and maintenance
  • Control measurements for settlement - monitoring
  • 3D modeling for machine control
  • Quantities calculation based on as-built and design measurements according to AMA 10 & MER 13
  • Orthophoto maps and photos using drone technology
  • Quantities estimation based on drone flights (stock piles volumes)
  • Documentation and Monthly payment certificates works
